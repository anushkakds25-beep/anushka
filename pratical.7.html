Mahatma Gandhi

Father of the Nation

About

Mahatma Gandhi, born on October 2, 1869, was a leader of India's independence movement against British rule. He is known for his philosophy of non-violence (Ahimsa) and truth (Satyagraha).

Major Contributions

• Inspired movements for civil rights worldwide. • Promoted self-reliance and simplicity. • Organized Non-Cooperation and Quit India Movements.

Famous Quote

"Be the change that you wish to see in the world."
